1. Controller

RedPOS (Red Soluciones / redsoluciones.com.pe) processes RedPOS Service data. Contact: jcefe.2920@gmail.com.

2. What is and is not collected

On device: paired printers, paper/cut settings, local job history, custom ticket templates (incl. logos), SUNAT ticket settings/logo, license/code pass, Play subscription status, and (if you subscribe) the Google email used at checkout.

To RedPOS: activation-code redemption only when an API is configured. We do not upload receipt PDF/image/XML to a RedPOS server to print.

We do not use Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics, AdMob, or ad-tracking SDKs.

3. Artificial intelligence

This app does not use generative artificial intelligence or ML models on user content. ESC/POS raster, voucher cropping, and SUNAT XML parsing are traditional algorithms, not generative AI.

4. Third parties and APIs

  • Google Play Billing — monthly product redpos_ads_free_monthly
  • Google Sign-In — subscribe only
  • RedPOS codes API (HTTPS) — code redemption
  • url_launcher and local print plugins; SharedPreferences

On-screen notices and paper footers are first-party RedPOS ads (not AdMob).

5. Deletion

In-app: Help → Data & privacy → Delete local data. Play subscription: cancel in Google Play. Server/account: delete-account.html or email support (about 30 days).
